# City Kia

## Positioning

City Kia of Greater Orlando is an automotive dealer. Janica Merchant and Raul Gomila are principal on-camera figures. Andrea is Miss City Kia 2026 and a temporary campaign image who may appear selectively.

## Visual system

- Dominant white and black, with metallic silver/gray.
- Red is an accent for prices, CTAs, underlines, and urgency.
- Editorial-commercial mix: offers, model spotlights, catalogs, comparisons, hacks, and helpful buying content.
- Copy should be brief and digestible. Choose English or Spanish campaign by campaign; default to English for retail offer campaigns when not otherwise specified.
- Use official logos in `assets/city-kia-logo-dark.png` and `assets/city-kia-logo-white.png`.
- Use approved dealership references when showing the location.

## People

- Janica: use the identity rules and assets from the Janica profile; City Kia wardrobe may be more casual-business.
- Raul: use `assets/raul-reference.png`.
- Andrea: use `assets/andrea-reference.png`; do not replace her with another brunette or pageant contestant.
- Keep expressions warm and approachable; do not make Janica appear angry or annoyed.

## Vehicle accuracy

Verify the official year and model before generating or publishing. Approved model watchlist:

- Kia K4 and K4 Hatchback
- Kia K5
- 2026 Sorento
- 2026 Sportage
- 2026 Niro
- 2026 Carnival
- 2027 Telluride
- 2027 EV3
- 2027 Seltos
- EV6 and EV9

Never use an older model because it looks similar. Search official Kia sources when a current model reference is missing.

## Offers and compliance

- Treat every payment, APR, discount, term, and inventory statement as time-sensitive.
- Confirm campaign details before production and add required disclaimers where appropriate.
- Do not reuse a past offer without current confirmation.
