# EVA

## Positioning

EVA is AI sales and appointment software developed and commercialized by Nika Media. It combines CRM, WhatsApp, text automation, AI voice calls, follow-up, qualification, scheduling, dashboards, and sales visibility. Do not call it merely a digital product.

## Audience and conversion

- Primary audience: owners of insurance agencies and service businesses.
- Primary conversion: request an EVA demo or write `EVA` by DM.
- Lead with business outcomes, speed, appointments, conversion, visibility, and operational efficiency.

## Visual system

- Premium, dark, technological, editorial.
- Charcoal/dark gray base; warm ivory or beige supporting panels.
- Electric lime-yellow from the EVA logo as a controlled accent, never overwhelming.
- Clean dashboard motifs, message flows, metrics, diagrams, timelines, and strong typography.
- Use the official EVA logo from `assets/eva-logo-dark-lime.png`.
- Include `EVA · by Nika Media` or `Software desarrollado y comercializado por Nika Media` where appropriate.
- Before any EVA carousel, read `02_ARCHIVOS_ORIGINALES/brand-standards/eva-carousel-visual-guide.md` and use the images in `02_ARCHIVOS_ORIGINALES/brands:eva:carousel-references/` as the priority layout/art-direction reference (pass them to the generator as style references).
- Main rule: slide 1 always opens with a photo of the topic featuring a human face (Janica with official references, the public figure in the news using real photos only, or a hyperrealistic person conveying the emotion). Never a text-only cover. Faces may recur or vary mid/end of carousel.
- Editorial layering: oversized type partially overlapping people/objects, big numbers as protagonists, data visualization whenever the topic allows. Avoid rigid template-like layouts.
- The cover photo must show the headline's situation (see "Narrative photography" in `production-workflow.md`), not a standalone portrait. Most slides carry a photo; type overlaps people and objects.
- Finishing rule: never flat solid backgrounds (use photo, abstract geometric backgrounds 1.png/2.png, grain or torn-paper texture); keep thin tech/AI HUD details (guide lines, arrows, frames, progress bars, small color blocks) in the margins, never over faces or key words; headline letters partly pass behind the person while staying legible. See the "Regla de acabado" section of the visual guide.
- Source rule: verify every external statistic with a primary/official source before writing; show the source on the slide ("Fuente: CBO, junio 2024"); match the source's scope and tense to today's date; never leave placeholders like [VERIFICAR] — drop the number if it can't be verified.
- Color rule: photos in real, natural color by default (people, faces, phones, screens, offices). Black and white only on 1–2 slides per carousel for rhythm, never two in a row; Janica's photo and the CTA slide always in color.
- Photography must look human and photographic (real skin texture, natural hands/hair), never glossy AI stock.
- External logos (WhatsApp, Meta, Instagram, etc.) must be exact; never AI reinterpretations.

## Approved proof points

- One agency moved from approximately 20-25 manually scheduled appointments per day to approximately 70-80 daily with EVA.
- One reported week showed approximately 320 scheduled appointments.
- EVA responds immediately and increases contact speed and prospecting capacity.
- Any claim about doubling/tripling results must be framed as a client average/example, not a universal guarantee.
- Hide the client name `Viva Insurance` in dashboards or proof unless explicitly authorized.

## Content style

- News, metrics, operational risks, automation tips, sales follow-up, lead response speed, and CRM visibility.
- Hooks may be dramatic, but clarify scope early and keep facts accurate.
- Default carousel length: 6-7 slides.
