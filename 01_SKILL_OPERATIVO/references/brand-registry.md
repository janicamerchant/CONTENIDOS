When a pending brand is requested, do not borrow another brand's complete system. Collect, in small groups of questions:

1. Audience, objective, and offers.
2. Tone, language, content pillars, and CTA.
3. Palette, typography, layouts, and photo frequency.
4. Logos, approved people, product references, and prohibited elements.
5. Examples the user likes and dislikes.

After approval, update the brand profile rather than relying only on conversation memory.
